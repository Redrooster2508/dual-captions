# Dual Captions

A Chrome extension for learning languages from video. It shows two subtitle lines at once (the language being learnt, with a translation underneath) on YouTube, Netflix and any HTML5 video, and keeps a library of the words saved from them.

Why: a big interest in learning languages, and existing services' paywalls felt like a big restriction. This one is free, needs no account, and keeps everything in the browser.

- Repository: https://github.com/Redrooster2508/dual-captions
